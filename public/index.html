Frequently Asked Questions

Is PRIME AVAY affiliated with Garena?

No. PRIME AVAY is a fully independent service. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garena or Free Fire, and we do not guarantee any unban outcome.

How does the key system work?

After you choose a plan, you can generate unique keys in the format PRIME-XXXXX. Each key costs ₹300 from your portal balance. You then bind a key to your Free Fire UID; the binding stays active for exactly 7 days.

What are the plan limits?

Premium (₹999) allows up to 25 keys per day. Ultimate (₹1,999) allows up to 50 keys per day. Limits are reset daily and enforced on the server.

Is my password safe?

Passwords are never stored in plain text. We hash them with a modern salted key-derivation function, and sessions use httpOnly cookies.
